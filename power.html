How the rankings work

Every team is rated on run margin, adjusted for who it played. Beating a strong team by 3 counts for more than beating a weak team by 10.

  • Whole schedule network. All 3,894 games in the SOAR database since fall 2025 are included, along with 1,008 opponents we don't track, so every schedule connects.
  • Fall counts most. Games since Aug 1, 2026 count fully. Spring/summer 2026 counts half, and fall 2025 a quarter.
  • Strength of schedule, round by round. Round 1 rates teams on raw margins. Each later round re-rates every team using its opponents' latest ratings. We repeated this until the order stopped changing. The top 10 settled by round 14, and the full table by round 46.
  • Guardrails. Blowouts are capped at 10 runs. Teams with few games are pulled toward average. A game that appears in both teams' books counts once.

Track record: the higher-rated team won 77% of 967 head-to-head games between ranked teams. Rated only on games before Oct 1, it picked 77% of the 150 games played after.
