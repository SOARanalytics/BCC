SOAR Analytics Power Rankings

A strength-of-schedule–adjusted rating of every tracked U11 club, built from complete game logs and solved as a single model across the entire opponent network — not just win-loss records.

Published by SOAR Analytics · Season-final edition ·

Methodology

The rating. Each of the tracked teams receives a rating from a ridge-regularized Massey least-squares model: every game contributes an equation stating that the rating gap between two teams equals their run margin, and all equations are solved simultaneously across a -team opponent network. This rewards who you beat, not merely how often.

Guardrails. Run margins are capped at ± to prevent blowouts from distorting results, ridge regularization stabilizes teams with thin samples, and every record and run total is read directly from official game logs. Ratings are centered so 0 equals a league-average team.

Validation. Back-tested against head-to-head results, the higher-rated team won % of decisive meetings.

About this model. SOAR Analytics ratings use a ridge-regularized Massey system with margin capping and log-derived records. Same-team name changes are reconciled (Mid-Atlantic Canes Gold → Olney Pirates Williams; Sandy Spring Falcons → Olney Pirates Henig). Tournament titles are inferred from schedule structure (a Saturday two-game day followed by a next-day Sunday sweep of 2–3 wins).

Ratings are analytical estimates for informational purposes and reflect only games with recorded results. Not affiliated with any league or sanctioning body.
