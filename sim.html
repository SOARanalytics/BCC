How this was built

  • Data: soar.duckdb, games through Oct 18. All eight teams are scraped; about 29,000 half-innings from roughly 2,900 games feed the fit. Friday's result (Winchester 21, Stars Poppert 0) is locked into every simulation but was not used to re-rate the teams.
  • Model: runs per half-inning drawn from a negative binomial with team offense, team defense, pitcher and team-day effects, fit by HMC (2 chains × 800 draws, max team R-hat 1.009). Pitchers come off each coach's actual usage with weekend pitch limits.
  • Game rules: 6 innings, run rule 15 after 3 / 10 after 4 / 8 after 5, 90 minutes with no new inning. Pool ties count as half a win.
  • Seeding: W-L record, then fewest runs allowed, then a coin flip.
  • Bracket format assumed: Sunday semifinals 1 v 4 and 2 v 3 (Gold), 5 v 8 and 6 v 7 (Silver), then finals, with the 90-minute limit and extra innings to break ties. The schedule didn't list Sunday times.
  • Thin data: Prime Time Aces have only 6 fall games and Alexandria Reds 9, so their ratings lean on spring. Stars Poppert is a new fall team with 13 games total.
  • Team names: Copperheads = Falls Church "Coppers." Alexandria Reds = the Red Team.
